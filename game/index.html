LUMEN DUEL

A LANTERN-LIGHT CARD DUEL AGAINST MIRA

How to play: You and Mira take turns playing cards.
Higher card takes the trick and scores both cards.
Chain: answer the lead suit and score +5 extra.
Lantern cards are wild — they match any suit.
First to 50 points wins the duel.
Sun Moon Leaf Stone Lantern — wild

Click a card to select it, click again to play. Keys 1–7 select, Enter plays, R restarts, M mutes.
